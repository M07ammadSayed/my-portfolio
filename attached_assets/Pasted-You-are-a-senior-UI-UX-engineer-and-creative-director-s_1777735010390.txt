You are a senior UI/UX engineer and creative director specializing in premium developer portfolios. Your task is to fully audit and redesign the visual quality of this portfolio to reach the highest possible level of sophistication, elegance, and refinement — without overdoing anything. The goal is subtle magnetism: a visitor should feel pulled in and impressed by restraint, not bombarded by effects.

You have full autonomy to make any change you judge necessary — typography, colors, spacing, animations, layout rhythm, micro-interactions, scroll behavior, hover states, transitions, gradients, shadows, and visual hierarchy. Do NOT ask for confirmation on any decision. Make the call, implement it, move forward.

---

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

> "Less is more. Every element should earn its place. Elegance is achieved not when there is nothing left to add, but when there is nothing left to remove."

- Refine, don't decorate.
- Every animation must have a purpose — guide attention, not distract.
- Consistency in spacing, motion curves, and typographic scale is non-negotiable.
- The palette should feel intentional and curated, not random.
- Hierarchy should be immediately legible at a glance.

---

## TYPOGRAPHY

- Audit every font in use. Replace anything generic or mismatched.
- Establish a strict 2-font system: one display/heading font with strong character, one clean readable font for body text.
- Recommended pairings to evaluate: Clash Display / Inter, Cabinet Grotesk / DM Sans, Syne / Outfit, General Sans / Geist.
- Apply a fluid typographic scale (clamp-based if possible). No hard pixel jumps between breakpoints.
- Tighten letter-spacing on large headings. Increase line-height on body text for breathing room.
- Remove all instances of default system fonts unless intentionally used as a design choice.

---

## COLOR SYSTEM

- Audit the entire color palette. Identify and eliminate any unintentional color use.
- Define a minimal token system: background, surface, border, text-primary, text-secondary, accent, accent-muted.
- Ensure sufficient contrast ratios on all text (WCAG AA minimum).
- Gradients, if used, must feel intentional and subtle — no rainbow or harsh transitions.
- Dark mode (if applicable) should feel rich, not flat gray.

---

## ANIMATIONS & TRANSITIONS

- Every page-load element should have a staggered entrance animation (fade-up, fade-in, or reveal) — smooth, not bouncy.
- Scroll-triggered animations: use IntersectionObserver or equivalent. Animate only once per element. Timing: 0.4s–0.7s, ease-out or cubic-bezier curves only.
- Hover states on cards, buttons, and links must feel responsive and premium — subtle lift (translateY -2px to -4px), soft box-shadow expansion, or color shift.
- No abrupt transitions anywhere on the site. Every state change must be eased.
- Page transitions (if routing exists): implement a clean fade or slide transition.
- Remove or replace any animation that feels cheap, bouncy, or excessive.
- Do NOT use spin animations, pulse loops, or dramatic scale effects unless absolutely justified by context.

---

## SPACING & LAYOUT

- Audit all section padding and margins. Establish a consistent spacing scale (4px base, multiples: 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128).
- Sections must breathe. Increase vertical padding on hero and key sections.
- Grid alignment must be precise. No orphaned elements or inconsistent gutters.
- On mobile: reduce padding proportionally but maintain hierarchy.

---

## MICRO-DETAILS

- Scrollbar: style it to match the palette (thin, minimal).
- Text selection color: set to accent color.
- Focus rings: replace browser defaults with a styled version matching the design system.
- Cursor: if a custom cursor exists, ensure it's smooth (lerp-interpolated). If not, evaluate whether adding one improves the experience.
- Links and CTAs: must have clear, elegant hover and active states.
- Images/avatars: ensure consistent border-radius treatment and no layout shifts on load.

---

## TESTING PROTOCOL

After every change batch, run the following checks autonomously:

1. **Visual regression** — screenshot key sections, verify no layout breaks.
2. **Responsive check** — test at 375px, 768px, 1280px, 1440px breakpoints.
3. **Animation performance** — ensure no janky transitions or layout-triggering properties (avoid animating width/height; prefer transform and opacity).
4. **Console errors** — zero errors allowed after your changes.
5. **Contrast check** — verify text contrast on all backgrounds.
6. **Load check** — ensure no new assets are blocking render.

Fix any issues found during testing without asking. Only stop and report when all checks pass.
